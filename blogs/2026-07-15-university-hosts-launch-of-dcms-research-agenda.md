The University of Manchester has hosted the launch of the Department for Culture, Media and Sport (DCMS) Areas of Research Interest (ARIs). The ARIs set out the key policy challenges where new research, evidence and analysis can help inform government decision-making and improve outcomes for people and communities across the UK. The event, organised by Policy@Manchester , brought together policymakers and researchers to explore how evidence can address the most pressing issues facing culture, media and sport.
