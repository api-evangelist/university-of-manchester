A range of regeneration projects are underway in North Manchester, with over £6bn to be spent over the next 15-20 years. As the area starts to be transformed, researchers from the University of Manchester’s Urban Ageing Research Group have been working with older residents and community groups across North Manchester to understand their thoughts and experiences of regeneration.
