University Research Committee is pleased to announce the recipients of the Interdisciplinary Pump Priming Awards , established to support new collaborations that advance the ambitions of Manchester 2035 . Following a highly competitive call, which attracted 89 applications from across the University, eight projects have been selected for funding. The awarded projects align with the priority areas of the University’s Platforms .
