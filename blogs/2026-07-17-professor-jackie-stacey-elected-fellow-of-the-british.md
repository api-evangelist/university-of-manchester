Jackie Stacey , Professor of Cultural Studies at The University of Manchester, has been elected a Fellow of the British Academy, one of the highest honours awarded to scholars in the humanities and social sciences. Professor Stacey is among 92 distinguished academics made Fellows by the British Academy in 2026.
