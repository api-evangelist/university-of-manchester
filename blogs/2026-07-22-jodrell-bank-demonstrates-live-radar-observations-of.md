Radio astronomers and radar experts have teamed up to demonstrate how powerful radio telescopes, including the 76-m Lovell Telescope and e-MERLIN antennas operated by the University of Manchester at Jodrell Bank, can be used to improve our capabilities for tracking satellites and space debris in orbit.
