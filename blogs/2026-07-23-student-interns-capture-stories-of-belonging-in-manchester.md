A group of student interns from the University of Manchester worked in collaboration with SICK! Productions to create a short film exploring identity, belonging and what it means to make North Manchester home. Students from the School of Social Sciences and the School of Arts, Languages and Cultures have taken part in a creative internship with SICK!
