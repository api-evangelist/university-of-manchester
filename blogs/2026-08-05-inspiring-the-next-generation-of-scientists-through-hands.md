PhD researchers from the National Graphene Institute brought science to life during a hands-on outreach day at a local primary school. Through interactive experiments including Berocca rockets, lava lamps, scribble bots, graphite circuits and magic milk, pupils explored chemistry, physics and engineering in a fun and engaging way. The day concluded with a student-led science careers fair, inspiring curiosity, confidence and a passion for future careers in STEM
