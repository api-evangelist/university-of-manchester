The July 2026 issue of the Open Research Digest is now available , bringing together the latest news, updates and opportunities from Open Research at The University of Manchester. This month’s Open Research Perspective is from Dr Tristan Martin , who reflects on two years of Research Data Conversations . Tristan explores how the series has helped foster a community of practice around research data stewardship, FAIR data principles and Open Research, while creating opportunities for networking, collaboration and knowledge sharing across Manchester and beyond.
