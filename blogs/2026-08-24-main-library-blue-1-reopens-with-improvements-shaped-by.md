Students returning to campus this academic year will find an improved Blue 1 study space in Main Library following a summer refurbishment designed to enhance accessibility, technology provision and the overall study experience. The refurbishment was informed by feedback from students, who told us that access to power, accessibility and comfortable study environments are among the most important features of Library spaces. As a result, Blue 1 has been upgraded to better support a wide range of study needs and ways of learning.
