Our new showcase, ‘The Student Team Takeover’, is on display at Main Library from Tuesday, 1 September 2026. This fifth Main Library Showcase has been curated by members of the Library Student Team; an award-winning staff-student group consisting of undergraduate, postgraduate taught and research students who work collaboratively with Library staff to produce digital resources and deliver and facilitate workshops.
