The Office for Open Research is inviting applications for the 2026–27 cohort of the Open Research Fellowship Programme . The Fellowship gives colleagues dedicated time, funding and support to develop their Open Research expertise, enhance their career development and contribute to the University’s wider Open Research Programme . Up to six Fellows will receive 0.2 FTE salary buyout for ten months, from January to October 2027.
