The Manchester China Institute (MCI) at The University of Manchester successfully hosted the Manchester International Arbitration Training Programme (MIATP) from 1 to 7 September 2026, bringing together more than 40 participants from leading universities and legal institutions across China, Ireland and Hong Kong.
