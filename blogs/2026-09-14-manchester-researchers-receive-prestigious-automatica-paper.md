Researchers from The University of Manchester have won the Automatica Paper Prize Award , one of the most prestigious international honours in systems and control research. The award recognises their paper, Feedback Stability Analysis via Dissipativity with Dynamic Supply Rates , which introduces a new theoretical framework for analysing the stability of complex dynamical systems, advancing fundamental understanding in control theory.
