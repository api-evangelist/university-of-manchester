Over the past six months, we’ve begun receiving an increased number of enquiries from researchers about Perma.cc . Some publishers - particularly in Law and Social Science, where citations to non-scholarly sources are more common - now ask authors to cite web sources via a Perma.cc link. The underlying problem is “link rot”, where over time links to webpages stop working, due to websites being restructured or content being deleted.
