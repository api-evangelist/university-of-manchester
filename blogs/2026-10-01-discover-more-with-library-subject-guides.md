Library subject guides bring together recommended resources and support in one convenient place. Whether you are studying, teaching or developing learning materials, they can help you find relevant databases, eBooks, eJournals, Special Collections and academic skills support. Over the summer vacation, each Database, eBook and eJournal page on our 65 discipline-specific guides has been refreshed to spotlight new Library acquisitions, publications by University of Manchester teaching and research staff, and titles requested through the Order a Book service.
