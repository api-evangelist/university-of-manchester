University of Manchester-led researchers have unveiled a roadmap for making computer-generated evidence trustworthy enough to help regulators decide whether medical devices are safe and effective, alongside laboratory and clinical evidence. Published in the journal Device , the framework was developed by researchers from academia, industry and the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA), the UK’s regulator for medicines and medical devices.
