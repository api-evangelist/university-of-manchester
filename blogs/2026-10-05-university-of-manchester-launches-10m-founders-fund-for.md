The University of Manchester is launching a £10m Founders Fund backed by philanthropic donations to provide early-stage investment to students, staff, alumni and University-affiliated start-up and spin-out ventures. The University of Manchester has committed £2m. Alumnus and technology entrepreneur Kevin Lomax has gifted £1m to the fund.
