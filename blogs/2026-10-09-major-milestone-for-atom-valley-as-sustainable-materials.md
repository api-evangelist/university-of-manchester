Base build completes on the 30,000 sq ft Sustainable Materials & Manufacturing Centre, the first major development within Rochdale’s Atom Valley Innovation District. The centre will connect businesses with specialist facilities, research and investment, with independent analysis forecasting £107 million in economic value over ten years.
