Bahrul Ilmi Nasution, a doctoral researcher in the Department of Social Statistics at The University of Manchester, recently presented his research, Understanding Latent Flow Models for Tabular Data Synthesis: Targets, Paths, and Sampling , at the European Conference on Machine Learning and Principles and Practice of Knowledge Discovery in Databases (ECML PKDD 2026) in Italy.
