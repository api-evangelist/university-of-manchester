A University of Manchester bicentenary music fellow has created an immersive opera in which audience choices shape the story. Ghost of the AI Empire combines live performance, Japanese Noh theatre and interactive technology for one night only at the Cosmo Rodewald Concert Hall, Martin Harris Centre on Friday 6 November.
