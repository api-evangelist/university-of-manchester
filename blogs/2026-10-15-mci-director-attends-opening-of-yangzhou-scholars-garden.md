Manchester China Institute Director Professor Lei Chen attended the opening of Yangzhou Scholars’ Garden at RHS Garden Bridgewater in Salford. The garden celebrates Chinese heritage, international friendship and the legacy of Dr Lee Kai Hung, whose philanthropy helped establish MCI.
