Representatives from Renmin University of China Law School visited the Manchester China Institute on 9 October 2026 to discuss potential academic collaboration. The meeting explored opportunities for joint research, seminars, academic visits and exchanges, drawing on legal scholarship and interdisciplinary research on China.
