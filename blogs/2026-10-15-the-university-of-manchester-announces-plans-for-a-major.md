The University of Manchester plans to establish a major new centre in Bengaluru, creating a permanent platform to deepen its long-standing relationship with India The proposed centre will create new opportunities for students to gain practical experience, connect with employers and develop skills needed in industry The Centre will strengthen research collaboration, educational partnerships and innovation The University makes the announcement as part of first-ever Great North Mayoral Mission to India
